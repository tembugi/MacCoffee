# Changelog

## 0.2.5 – 2026-10-29
- The menu opens in the same place and at the right size every time, instead of every other time being cut off or too tall.

## 0.2.4 – 2026-10-29
- On macOS 27 the menu bar opens and closes the menu like any other: the cup stays highlighted while it's open, and clicking it again closes the menu.

## 0.2.3 – 2026-10-29
- Clicking the cup while the menu is open closes it, instead of closing and reopening it.

## 0.2.2 – 2026-10-29
- Turn All On and Turn All Off always look alike, so neither looks like the current state.

## 0.2.1 – 2026-10-29
- The menu closes when you click anywhere else or press Esc, and switches that are on show in blue.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-10-27
- Keep Screen On: with the lid closed, the screen can stay on at minimum brightness instead of turning off.
- The version number is shown in the menu.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-10-26
- First release.
